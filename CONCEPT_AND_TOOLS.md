1. Project Concept

Project Name: College Student Affairs Management System

Problem

College students often face difficulties when contacting the Student Affairs department, submitting requests, following up on their status, and checking the requirements of different services. Managing these requests manually can also make it difficult for staff to organize tickets, verify payments, and track their work efficiently.

Solution

The College Student Affairs Management System is designed to provide a centralized platform that connects students, Student Affairs staff, and administrators.

The system allows students to:

- Create service tickets and submit their requests.
- Track their tickets using a tracking code.
- Attach required documents and payment receipts.
- Receive email notifications about ticket updates and resolution comments.
- Access knowledge base articles and regulations.

The system allows Student Affairs staff to:

- View and filter submitted tickets by category.
- Update ticket statuses.
- Resolve student requests.
- Verify payment receipts by checking transaction information.

The system also includes an AI Agent to assist with scanning payment receipts, extracting transaction IDs, and identifying other relevant receipt details to support the payment verification process.

Administrators can:

- Manage knowledge base articles.
- Manage staff accounts and permissions.
- Generate performance reports.
- Export ticket reports as CSV files.

Target Audience

The main target users of the system are:

1. Students: To submit requests, upload documents, and track their tickets.
2. Student Affairs Staff: To manage student requests, verify payments, and resolve tickets.
3. Administrators: To manage staff accounts, knowledge base content, permissions, and system performance reports.

2. Main System Features

- User authentication and account management.
- Ticket creation and tracking.
- Ticket categorization and status management.
- Ticket comments and history tracking.
- File attachments and payment receipt submission.
- AI-assisted receipt scanning and data extraction.
- Payment verification.
- Email notifications.
- Knowledge base management.
- Staff account and permission management.
- Performance reports and CSV export.
